Hello! I am a PhD candidate at the Heinz College, Carnegie Mellon University. I am fortunate to be advised by [Prof. Peter Zhang](https://www.heinz.cmu.edu/faculty-research/profiles/zhang-peter). 

I work on extending the practical capability of decision-making under uncertainty. Making robust decisions typically costs decision-makers more computation; I develop methods that reduce this computational overhead and bridge the gap between decision-making under uncertainty theory, computation, and practice. To this end, my research has two interacting themes:

- I reveal new ways to understand the structure of optimal decisions that are robust against uncertainty.
  
- Leveraging the new structural insights, I develop new faster algorithms to compute robust decisions.
